    • Information We Collect

  • We collect personal data through the following parts of our website and services:

  • Contact form submissions: when you contact us through our website, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content so we can respond to your inquiry.

  • Job applications and careers: if you apply for a role with us, we collect the information you provide as part of your application (such as your contact details, CV, and application history) to evaluate your candidacy.

  • Customer and client portal: clients who use our portal provide account and contact information necessary to deliver and manage our facilities management services.

  • Operational and notification emails: we send transactional and workflow notification emails (including interactive emails) to employees and clients related to tasks, approvals, and other service-related activity, using delivery infrastructure that records standard delivery metadata (e.g. delivery status).

  • The ONE FM mobile app: employees who use the app provide location, face video, document photos and device details, as described in "The ONE FM Mobile App" below.

    • How We Use Your Information

  • We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our website;
    Evaluate and process job applications;
    Deliver, manage, and support the facilities management services we provide to our clients;
    Operate internal workflow and task notifications relevant to the services we deliver;
    Record employee attendance and confirm the identity of employees who check in through the mobile app;
    Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

    • How We Share Your Information

  • We do not sell personal data. We share personal data only with service providers who help us operate our business, for example our email delivery provider (Google Workspace) used to send operational and notification emails, and Google Cloud and Google Firebase, which host the mobile app's data and deliver its notifications. We share data only to the extent necessary for them to provide that service to us. We may also disclose information where required by law.

    • The ONE FM Mobile App

  • The ONE FM mobile app is for ONE FM employees only. ONE FM creates every account. You cannot sign up in the app. This section lists what the app collects, why, and how long we keep it.

    • What the App Collects and Why

  • Data When Why
    Precise location Only when you check in or check out. The app does not track you in the background. To confirm you are at your assigned site.
    Short face video When you enrol, and at each check-in and check-out. To confirm that the person checking in is you. We treat face data as biometric data.
    Photos of documents When you attach them to a leave or resignation request. To support the request you submit.
    Notification token and device type (Android or iOS) When you log in. To send you work notifications. Deleted when you log out.
    Employee ID and profile details Whenever you use the app. To link your actions to your employee record.
  • We do not use app data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

    • Face Data

  • At enrolment, the app records a short video. Our face recognition service extracts still images of your face and a face template from it.
    At each check-in, the app records a short video and compares it with your enrolment images. The check-in video is deleted after the comparison.
    If a check-in video fails the liveness check, we keep it for up to 30 days to investigate errors, then delete it.
    Your enrolment images and face template are kept while you are employed and deleted when your employment ends.

    • Where App Data Is Processed and Who Receives It

  • App data is stored and processed on Google Cloud servers in Europe. We do not share it outside ONE FM, except with service providers that host our systems (Google Cloud, and Google Firebase for notifications), who process it only on our instructions. We may disclose data when the law requires it.

    • How Long We Keep App Data

  • Data Kept for
    Check-in location (attendance record) As part of your attendance record, for as long as Kuwaiti law requires us to keep employment records.
    Check-in location (site distance check log) 30 days
    Failed check-in videos 30 days
    Enrolment images and face template Until your employment ends
    Document photos As part of your HR record, for as long as Kuwaiti law requires us to keep employment records.
    Notification token Until you log out

    • Data Retention

  • We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements that apply to us. The periods for mobile app data are listed above.

    • Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@one-fm.com. Employees can also ask their HR department. We reply within 30 days. Some data, such as attendance and payroll records, must be kept by law, and we will tell you if we cannot delete it.

    • Cookies

  • Our website uses only cookies necessary for the site to function correctly. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

    • Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

    • Contact Us

  • If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@one-fm.com.

    • البيانات التي يجمعها التطبيق ولماذا

  • البيانات متى لماذا
    الموقع الدقيق فقط عند تسجيل الحضور أو الانصراف. لا يتتبعك التطبيق في الخلفية. للتأكد من وجودك في موقع العمل المحدد لك.
    فيديو قصير للوجه عند التسجيل الأول، وعند كل تسجيل حضور أو انصراف. للتأكد من أن الشخص الذي يسجل الحضور هو أنت. نتعامل مع بيانات الوجه على أنها بيانات بيومترية.
    صور المستندات عند إرفاقها بطلب إجازة أو استقالة. لدعم الطلب الذي تقدمه.
    رمز الإشعارات ونوع الجهاز (أندرويد أو iOS) عند تسجيل الدخول. لإرسال إشعارات العمل إليك. يُحذف عند تسجيل الخروج.
    الرقم الوظيفي وبيانات الملف الشخصي عند استخدام التطبيق. لربط ما تقوم به بسجلك الوظيفي.
  • لا نستخدم بيانات التطبيق لأغراض إعلانية، ولا نبيعها.

    • بيانات الوجه

  • عند التسجيل الأول، يسجل التطبيق فيديو قصيرًا تستخرج منه خدمة التعرف على الوجه صورًا ثابتة لوجهك ونموذجًا للوجه.
    عند كل تسجيل حضور، يسجل التطبيق فيديو قصيرًا ويقارنه بصور التسجيل الأول. يُحذف فيديو الحضور بعد المقارنة.
    إذا فشل فيديو الحضور في اختبار التحقق من الحيوية، نحتفظ به لمدة أقصاها 30 يومًا لدراسة الأخطاء، ثم نحذفه.
    نحتفظ بصور التسجيل الأول ونموذج الوجه طوال مدة عملك، ونحذفها عند انتهاء خدمتك.

    • أين تُعالج بيانات التطبيق ومن يتلقاها

  • تُخزَّن بيانات التطبيق وتُعالَج على خوادم Google Cloud في أوروبا. لا نشاركها خارج ون إف إم، إلا مع مزودي الخدمات الذين يستضيفون أنظمتنا (Google Cloud، وGoogle Firebase للإشعارات)، والذين يعالجونها وفق تعليماتنا فقط. قد نفصح عن البيانات عندما يلزمنا القانون بذلك.
